# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- Papers are now their PDF's text (or, for a web article, its markdown): the buttons and Reveal in Library open the text, and switch between the PDF and the text.
- Search Library Text searches the papers' text.
- A paper is found whatever Unicode form a synced folder gives its name.

## 0.1.0

- Open a paper by its title, with its topic, year and summary; Enter opens the PDF, and buttons open the markdown or the notes.
- Switch between a paper's PDF, markdown, notes and topic index.
- Search the papers' text.
