Conversion errors are recorded and fixed at their cause

No converter gets every paper right. Each problem is recorded once, filed under its cause, and fixed in the converter.
1Recorded

While reading a paper, or when a session notices one, each problem becomes a record: where, what, and whether the findings rest on it.

→
2Filed under a cause

Each record goes under a ticket, one per cause, which says what it takes and how many papers it touches.

/triage-conversions↑
→
3Fixed in the converter

A ticket is fixed once, with a test, so every paper added later gets it.

/fix-conversions↑
→
4Converted again

Overnight, every paper the ticket touches is converted again. Only notes whose key content was broken are read again.
