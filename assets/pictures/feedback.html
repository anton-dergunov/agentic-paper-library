Every paper read tests the converter

the model that reads a paper for its note also reports what the conversion broke, and recurring flaws are fixed in the converter

1Read

paperlib read <area>
Each note has a line: conversion: ok, what is broken and on which page, or key-content-broken: when the findings rest on it.
→

2Collect

paperlib conversion-issues
Every reported problem is copied into the catalog: one list for the library.
→

3Fix at the source

/fix-conversions
A recurring flaw goes to the converter, with a fixture in the tests. A single one is fixed from the PDF, or marked wont-fix.
→

4Reconvert

paperlib reconvert
The whole library, resumably, then checked: known symptoms, shrunken papers, formulas, missing pages.
→

5Read again, rarely

only key-content-broken
A note is rewritten only when its paper's key content was broken and is now fixed.
