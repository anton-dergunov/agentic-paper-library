- `damaged:` when something this note or a comparison with other papers needs is missing or unreliable: a results table that is absent, flattened past reading, or has values in the wrong rows or columns; marking lost that carries a claim (bold for the significant results, shading that separates conditions); a missing section; a truncated body; the paper's main equation unreadable; numbers that look wrong.
- `minor:` when the meaning is still recoverable from the text: an equation or algorithm garbled but explained in the prose, a table that can be read by order, reading order scrambled on one page, lost formatting that carries no claim.
