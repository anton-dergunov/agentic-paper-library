# Reading list: foundations, data-centric and trustworthy ML

The library's foundations folders hold the classic boosting and dimensionality-reduction papers (XGBoost, gradient boosting machines, t-SNE, UMAP, DBSCAN, SMOTE), the first tabular deep-learning and TabPFN papers, a handful of evaluation and MCMC tutorials, and the Snorkel line of weak supervision. The data-centric folders have Confident Learning, label-error and ChatGPT-as-annotator papers and a few augmentation surveys; trustworthy-ml has a solid privacy and federated-learning core (DP-SGD, FedAvg, secure aggregation, membership inference) but only two fairness papers and three AI-safety papers. This list fills the gaps: the remaining classical references (LightGBM, random forests, SVMs, clustering), the recent tabular line (TabArena, TabM, TabICL, TabPFN-2.5/3, Mitra), statistical practice for comparing models and quoting uncertainty (Demsar, leakage, cross-validation, conformal prediction, Bayesian workflow), the full weak-supervision foundation (data programming through LLM-era labeling functions), annotation quality and aggregation (Dawid-Skene, disagreement, noisy-label benchmarks, and the 2023-2025 evidence on LLM annotators), distribution-shift and dataset-bias references, the fairness canon with fair-ranking work relevant to search and recommendation, privacy attacks, auditing and DP at LLM scale, and general AI-safety (alignment framings, reward hacking, control, safety cases, the 2026 International AI Safety Report). LLM-specific safety and privacy work is left to the llm/safety-and-privacy list. Non-arXiv papers carry a DOI or publisher/author URL (some publishers block scripted fetches; DOIs were checked to resolve).

Overlap notes: papers that other area lists may also propose are marked in "Why" or in the considered table; the add script will skip anything already added.
