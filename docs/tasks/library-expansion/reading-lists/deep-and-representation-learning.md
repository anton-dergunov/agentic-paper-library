# Reading list: deep learning and representation learning

The library's deep-learning folders are strongest on classic training mechanics (Adam, AdamW, cyclical and warm-restart schedules, batch norm, layer norm, dropout, weight decay, label smoothing), the lottery-ticket and grokking literature, Hinton-style distillation and BERT distillation, deep ensembles and MC-dropout, and the first generation of architectures (ResNet, DenseNet, LSTM, Transformer, GELU). Representation learning has SimCLR, MoCo, BYOL, SwAV, Barlow Twins, VICReg, DINO/DINOv2/DINOv3, MAE and CPC, and for embeddings word2vec, doc2vec, SBERT, SimCSE, Matryoshka, E5-style contrastive pretraining and ArcFace/FaceNet. The list fills the gaps: the rest of the CNN and sequence-model lineage (VGG, Inception v1, attention-based seq2seq, MobileNets, EfficientNet, Neural ODEs, Deep Sets) and the 2024-2026 residual-stream work (hyper-connections); the optimizer picture after AdamW (large-batch and second-order optimizers, critical batch size, schedule-free and constant-LR schedules, muP-style transfer, and 2025-26 optimizer benchmarks); modern normalization (QK-norm, Peri-LN, DyT, depth scaling) and mixing regularizers; core generalization theory (rethinking generalization, double descent, NTK, muP/feature learning, spectral and simplicity bias, neural collapse, plasticity, 2025 grokking theory); general compression (Deep Compression, pruning, quantization, mixed precision, distillation surveys); adversarial and OOD robustness (FGSM, PGD, OOD scores, OpenOOD, dataset-shift calibration, underspecification); JEPA-family and theory-led SSL; and embeddings for retrieval (instruction-aware and LLM-based embedders, MMTEB, the limits of single-vector retrieval, cosine-similarity and inversion pitfalls, metric-learning reality checks). LLM-specific architecture, efficiency and quantization papers are left to the LLM lists (llm/architecture and llm/efficiency already hold much of them), and dense-retrieval embedders (E5, BGE-M3, NV-Embed, Gemini, Qwen3) are already in search-and-ranking.
