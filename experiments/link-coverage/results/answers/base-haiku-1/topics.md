**Short answer:** this paper doesn't name a default. Page 3 is the survey's introduction and the topic-model setup (Figure 2, the LDA notation). The survey itself is a map, not a recommendation. What the library supports is a judgement built from pages 9–10 and the FASTopic note. My pick is FASTopic, with BERTopic as the practical baseline.

- **Survey, p. 9:** the clustering family (Top2Vec, BERTopic) and LLM prompting (TopicGPT) are covered. The survey notes that the clustering methods only give topics and don't infer doc-topic distributions "as required" ([p. 9](http://pdf.invalid/nlp/topic-models/A%20Survey%20on%20Neural%20Topic%20Models.%20Methods,%20Applications,%20and%20Challenges.pdf?page=9)). BERTopic is the widely used one of these.
- **Survey, p. 10:** FASTopic is presented as the newer paradigm. It uses frozen pretrained document embeddings and optimal-transport relations to learned topic and word embeddings, and the survey calls it "a neat and efficient paradigm" ([p. 10](http://pdf.invalid/nlp/topic-models/A%20Survey%20on%20Neural%20Topic%20Models.%20Methods,%20Applications,%20and%20Challenges.pdf?page=10)).
- **FASTopic note** (the paper itself, from the agent note; check the PDF before quoting): it reports the best topic diversity on all six datasets and the best C_V on five of six. Running times are 10–60 s, against 30–115 s for BERTopic on the same datasets. The baselines were the authors' own runs, and the evaluation is entirely automatic.
- **Caveat:** the topic-quality numbers rest on automated coherence (C_V). The coherence paper in this topic folder argues that this metric was never validated for neural topic models, so the margins should be read with that in mind.

**Recommendation:** use FASTopic as the default when you want a neural topic model that is fast and stable with pretrained embeddings, and BERTopic as the baseline you measure it against. Neither is validated with human judgement in these papers, so for a decision that matters, run a small human or word-intrusion check on your own corpus.
