# arXiv-HTML tables flattened, shifted or split

`engine` `reconvert` · html · open

**Broken.** A table from arXiv's HTML arrives with its header spans shifted, rows split or merged, cells out of place, or a stray character in a cell; most stay readable by order.

**Fix.** Not known yet: a catch-all filed by the 2026-10-16 migration. Sample its records, group them into sub-causes (header spans, split tables, cell artefacts), and give each a ticket of its own; some may already be fixed by the 2026-10-08 table work.

**What it gives.** Tables an agent can read cell by cell instead of by order.

**Cost.** Unknown until its sub-causes are found.

**Examples.** mixup Tables 4–5; Chain-of-Verification Table 1 (header spans shifted); "Text Embeddings Reveal (Almost) As Much As Text" Table 3.
