# Old PDFs with a broken text layer

`by-hand` `reconvert` · pdf · open

**Broken.** Old PDFs lose ligatures and, for LSTM, headings and equations; scans have an OCR text layer that garbles headings, symbols and numbers.

**Fix.** Replace each PDF with a cleaner copy, then reconvert.

**What it gives.** These classic papers readable without the PDF.

**Cost.** By hand: find a cleaner PDF of each, then reconvert it.

**Examples.** "Long Short-Term Memory"; "The power of two random choices"; "The Anatomy of a Large-Scale Hypertextual Web Search Engine" (a scan; its two tables were rewritten by hand); "The Use of MMR, Diversity-Based Reranking…" (OCR reads λ as "~", "X" or "k", Table 3 collapsed).
