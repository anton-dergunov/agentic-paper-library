# Title and author lines at the top of the body garbled

`engine` · any · wont-fix

**Broken.** The heading or the author block at the top of a paper's body is wrong: a word of the title replaced by a logo, a PDF title that differs from the library's, affiliation marks scrambled or replaced by †, empty affiliation footnotes.

**Fix.** Write the heading from the frontmatter title and drop the author block, or leave it.

**What it gives.** Little: the frontmatter carries the title and authors an agent reads.

**Cost.** Engine: small, for lines nothing reads.

**Examples.** OpenThoughts (title logo); DAPO, "In-context Autoencoder", "SFT Memorizes, RL Generalizes" (author blocks); "The Use of MMR" (the PDF's own title).
