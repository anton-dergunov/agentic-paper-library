# PDF equations read wrongly by the equation model

`engine` `reconvert` · pdf · open

**Broken.** An equation of a PDF paper is present but garbled: symbols misread, parts duplicated, lemma statements in fragments. Unlike `pdf-equation-unparsable`, the TeX parses.

**Fix.** Not known yet: a catch-all. Sample its records against the page images; a better equation model, a larger crop, or a second reading where the first looks wrong are candidates.

**What it gives.** Derivations an agent can follow without the PDF.

**Cost.** Unknown until its sub-causes are found.

**Examples.** "From RankNet to LambdaRank to LambdaMART" §7 (p. 16); "Adaptive Subgradient Methods…" Lemmas 9, 14, 16; "L2 Regularization versus Batch and Weight Normalization" §3.1.
